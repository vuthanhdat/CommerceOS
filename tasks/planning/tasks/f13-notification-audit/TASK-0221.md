## Objective
Deliver required privileged/security evidence reliably for Tenant lifecycle, Membership/role administration, refund review and other covered actions without best-effort logging.

## Scope
Producer durable Audit intent/outbox contracts; versioned evidence messages; Audit consumer queue/DLQ/inbox; initial named routes for platform suspend/reactivate, Membership/role security changes, non-disclosing security-significant denials and refund approval/rejection as dependencies exist.

## Out of scope
Routing every ordinary CRUD event to Audit, Audit changing producer result.

## Inputs
PD-033, producer tasks, ADR-006.

## Expected changes
Producer outbox intents/integration infrastructure/Audit consumer/tests.

## Implementation steps
Define minimal stable evidence contract, atomically persist producer intent with business action where required, route to Audit, dedupe, test producer/consumer failures and redrive.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Covered accepted privileged action has durable Audit delivery intent tied to the owner commit.
- [ ] Required security-significant rejections are captured without exposing target details to merchant views.
- [ ] Audit delivery failure does not roll back already committed owner fact and is recoverable.
- [ ] Duplicate/redrive creates one logical evidence entry.
- [ ] Actor/context/reason/outcome/correlation are preserved safely.

## Verification
Contract/unit + LocalStack route/queue/DLQ + producer integration + harness.

## Architecture / security / reliability / observability / local runtime
Reliable async evidence, not synchronous business authority. Protected logs/evidence avoid secrets.

## Risks and edge cases
Some producer tasks may not yet exist; integrate named available routes incrementally and track follow-ups.

## Open questions
Do not invent broader audit coverage beyond domain policy during this task.

## Completion report
Record covered route inventory.
