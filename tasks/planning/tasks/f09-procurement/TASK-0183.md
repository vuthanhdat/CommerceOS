## Objective
Record one MVP SupplierInvoice and one full SupplierPayment per fully received PO, preserving explicit variance approval and merchant-attested payment evidence.

## Scope
Invoice creation after full receipt; supplier reference/date/Money; exact-match auto acceptance; variance approval path; one invoice claim; full payment after invoice; external reference/date; immutable facts/outbox.

## Out of scope
Partial/multiple invoices/payments, bank execution, tax accounting.

## Inputs
PD-029, Procurement domain baseline.

## Expected changes
Procurement Domain/Application/Infrastructure and tests.

## Implementation steps
Implement invoice/payment aggregates or owned records, PO gates, Money validation, variance state/approval authorization, uniqueness/idempotency and facts.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Invoice cannot be recorded before PO is fully received.
- [ ] Exactly one SupplierInvoice per PO in MVP.
- [ ] Exact match may auto-accept; mismatch requires explicit approved merchant action and preserves variance evidence.
- [ ] SupplierPayment requires invoice and is full-payment evidence only.
- [ ] Payment is merchant attestation, never bank execution.
- [ ] Facts are immutable/idempotent and ready for Accounting consumers.

## Verification
Domain/persistence/authorization/idempotency/harness.

## Architecture / security / reliability / observability / local runtime
No banking secrets. Tenant-scoped and traceable.

## Risks and edge cases
Duplicate supplier invoice references are not assumed globally unique unless domain says so.

## Open questions
Exact approval role should follow ordinary domain authorization; if materially unclear during implementation, surface before guessing.

## Completion report
