## Objective
Record immutable confirmed physical receipt evidence and corrections as new compensating records rather than editing history.

## Scope
GoodsReceipt draft/confirm; line/quantity validation against PO policy; immutable confirmation; correction reference/effect; derived net received quantity; Inventory application status Pending/Applied/NeedsAttention as operational integration view; fact outbox.

## Out of scope
Inventory mutation inside Procurement transaction, destructive receipt edit, supplier invoice before full receipt.

## Inputs
PD-028 and Procurement fact catalog.

## Expected changes
Procurement aggregate/persistence/contracts/tests.

## Implementation steps
Implement receipt identity/state/revision, immutable confirmation, correction command/evidence, source-idempotent fact, derived PO receipt status.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Confirmed receipt cannot be edited/deleted.
- [ ] Error correction creates linked compensating evidence.
- [ ] PO received quantity derives from accepted receipt/correction evidence, not Inventory application success.
- [ ] GoodsReceiptRecorded does not claim Inventory/accounting effects.
- [ ] Duplicate confirm/correction intent cannot duplicate logical evidence.
