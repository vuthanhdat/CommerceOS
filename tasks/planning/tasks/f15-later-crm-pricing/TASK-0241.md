## Objective
Resolve and record the product/domain semantics for the first Pricing & Promotion slice before any Pricing application code is written.

This task is a product/domain design gate. It must leave a deterministic policy that a Technical Architect and Builder can implement without inventing promotion behavior.

## Context
CommerceOS reserves Pricing & Promotion as the owner of authoritative offer/discount rules. Catalog owns canonical Product, base selling price and sellability; Sales owns the immutable accepted Order snapshot. `PD-013` excludes manual authoritative guest-checkout discounts and requires future discount behavior to be introduced through Pricing rather than smuggled into Sales.

The previous version of this task mixed product decisions, technical design and implementation in one XL task. It was split so TASK-0241 resolves product/domain meaning, TASK-0242 resolves technical design, and TASK-0243 implements the approved slice.

## Resolved design — PD-054

The Product Owner approved the first-slice direction and delegated resolution of its remaining lifecycle/time/history details in this TASK-0241 pass. The authoritative resolution is `PD-054 — First scheduled Product promotional-price policy`, with the detailed domain baseline in `docs/domains/pricing-promotion.md`.

The resulting first slice is deliberately narrow:

1. One scheduled Promotion targets one Tenant-owned Product and applies to all public-storefront shoppers.
2. The Promotion stores an explicit final promotional unit price in VND whole đồng; percentage/fixed-reduction formulas are out of scope.
3. There is no persisted merchant-editable Draft in the first slice. A successful schedule command accepts immutable terms; changes require cancellation plus a new Promotion identity.
4. `Upcoming`, `Active`, and `Expired` are temporal interpretations, not activation/expiry commands.
5. Effective periods are finite, start-inclusive/end-exclusive `[EffectiveFrom, EffectiveUntil)`, may begin now or in the future, and cannot be backdated.
6. Tenant IANA timezone is used for merchant-facing scheduling/display, but accepted terms resolve to unambiguous instants; ambiguous/nonexistent local wall-clock times are rejected.
7. Owner/Admin may schedule and cancel; Staff/Viewer may not mutate Pricing.
8. Promotion periods for the same Tenant + Product may be adjacent but may not overlap. There is no stacking or priority engine.
9. Scheduling requires a currently Published Product and promotional Money `>= 0` and strictly below current Catalog base price. Zero promotional price is allowed when base price is positive.
10. Pricing never overrides Catalog sellability. During an otherwise-active Promotion, effective price is `min(current Catalog base price, scheduled promotional unit price)`.
11. A later Catalog base-price decrease to equal/below the promotional price makes the Promotion temporarily non-beneficial rather than increasing shopper price, cancelling the schedule, or blocking the Catalog edit.
12. Unpublished Products are not sellable; republishing before the Promotion ends may make the Promotion effective again. Archived Products remain terminal.
13. Future Promotions are not publicly exposed before start. When actually applied, public display may show base price, effective promotional price, a generic promotion indication and the end instant.
14. The existing `PD-011` checkout rule remains authoritative: any difference between the shopper-confirmed estimate and current effective price requires explicit reconfirmation and no Order is placed for that attempt.
15. Sales keeps immutable accepted commercial evidence independent of mutable Pricing state: accepted base unit price, effective unit price, applied PromotionId when applicable, accepted promotional unit price when applied, and authoritative pricing-evaluation instant.
16. Scheduled/cancelled/expired Promotion records are retained non-destructively. Historical Orders are never repriced by later Catalog or Promotion changes.
17. Accounting and refunds use the effective amount actually accepted on the Sales Order. No contra-revenue/discount account is introduced for this slice.
18. No new Pricing-specific subscription entitlement or plan-name gate is introduced for this slice.

## Out of scope

- Promotion-specific plan packaging.
- Gross-discount/contra-revenue accounting presentation.
- Repricing historical Orders.

## Architecture / security / reliability / observability / local runtime
TASK-0241 chooses no persistence or runtime mechanism. Tenant ownership, Merchant Access authority, immutable schedule semantics, interval behavior, non-overlap and owner boundaries are product/domain constraints only. TASK-0242 must design contracts, persistence/concurrency enforcement, observability and LocalStack topology without changing PD-054 for technical convenience.

## Risks and edge cases
- Treating the scheduled final price as a generic discount primitive would silently expand scope.
- A best-effort read-before-write overlap check would not satisfy the domain invariant under concurrency; TASK-0242 must design protected enforcement.
- Mutable Pricing history must never become required to explain/refund/account for an accepted Order.
- Infrastructure/server timezone must not leak into business scheduling semantics.
