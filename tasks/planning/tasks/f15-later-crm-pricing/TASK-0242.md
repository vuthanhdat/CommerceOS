## Objective
Translate the resolved `PD-054` Pricing/Promotion product/domain semantics into a concrete technical design that a Builder can implement without inventing ownership, persistence, concurrency or integration behavior.

This task is technical design only. It must not implement Pricing application code.

## Context
TASK-0241 is complete and its product/domain semantics are authoritative through `PD-054` and `docs/domains/pricing-promotion.md`. No first-slice Pricing product question remains open.

TASK-0234 remains an independent prerequisite because the technical design should inspect the implemented post-hardening modular architecture before choosing concrete module, persistence and integration mechanisms. The architecture audit may affect mechanisms but may not reopen PD-054 business policy.

Pricing remains an explicit bounded-context authority: Catalog owns base price and Product sellability, Pricing owns current effective promotional-price decisions, Storefront/Checkout consumes authoritative pricing, Sales stores the accepted immutable commercial snapshot, and Accounting/Refund continue from accepted Sales facts rather than reading Pricing history.

## Fixed product/domain constraints from PD-054

Technical design must preserve all of the following:

- one Promotion targets one Tenant + Product and all public-storefront shoppers;
- accepted terms are immutable; no persisted merchant-editable Draft exists in the first slice;
- changing terms means cancel old + schedule a new Promotion identity;
- effective interval is finite `[EffectiveFrom, EffectiveUntil)`, no backdated schedule;
- Tenant-local scheduling must resolve to unambiguous instants;
- Owner/Admin mutate Pricing; Staff/Viewer do not;
- accepted intervals for one Tenant + Product cannot overlap; adjacent intervals are allowed;
- scheduling requires currently Published Product and promotional VND Money `>= 0` and `<` current Catalog base price;
- Pricing never grants sellability;
- when otherwise active, effective unit price is `min(current Catalog base price, scheduled promotional unit price)`;
- a non-beneficial Promotion still occupies its accepted interval unless cancelled;
- cancellation truncates future eligibility but never rewrites accepted Orders;
- public display exposes promotion data only while the Promotion actually lowers the current sellable Product price;
- PD-011 repricing/reconfirmation applies to every effective-price change;
- Sales snapshot must be independently sufficient for historical commercial truth;
- Accounting/refunds use accepted Sales effective amounts;
- no new Pricing-specific subscription entitlement/plan-name gate exists in this slice;
- coupons, percentage/fixed-reduction rules, manual discounts, stacking/priority, customer/segment pricing, price lists and broad promotion-rule engine behavior remain out of scope.

## Scope
Design the smallest implementation architecture for the first scheduled Product promotional-price slice:

- Pricing module boundary and allowed dependencies.
- Promotion aggregate/entity/value-object shape consistent with immutable accepted terms and cancellation/history semantics.
- Tenant-owned persistence/access patterns and concurrency enforcement for non-overlapping Product promotion periods.
- Command contracts for Owner/Admin schedule/cancel operations.
- Producer-owned Pricing query/decision contract used by Storefront/Checkout.
- Contract fields required to express Catalog base price, effective price, applied Promotion identity/provenance and evaluation timestamp without leaking persistence representation.
- Behavior when no Promotion is actually applied, including active-but-non-beneficial schedules.
- Trusted time source and Tenant business-time conversion boundary.
- Checkout integration with existing repricing/reconfirmation behavior.
- Sales Order snapshot integration so accepted pricing provenance is immutable and sufficient for later explanation/refund/accounting.
- Public projection/display contract matching PD-054.
- Failure semantics: Pricing unavailable, stale/conflicting mutation, overlapping schedule, Product no longer eligible, concurrent schedule/cancel operations and time-boundary races.
- Observability/correlation requirements.
- LocalStack-compatible persistence/resource mapping and focused verification strategy.
- ADR decision if the design introduces a material new architecture/topology choice not already covered by accepted ADRs.

## Out of scope
- Application/domain/infrastructure implementation.
- Re-deciding PD-054 lifecycle, price, role, public-display, Accounting/refund or entitlement semantics for implementation convenience.
- Coupons, manual discounts, percentage/fixed discount engine, stacking/priority engine, customer segments, Category/cart/order-wide promotions or price lists.
- Reading Catalog or Sales private persistence directly.
- Making Pricing a sellability/publication authority.
- Repricing historical Orders.
- Extracting Pricing to a separate deployment unit without measured pressure and an accepted ADR.

## Architecture / security / reliability / observability / local runtime
- Trusted Tenant context is mandatory for merchant Pricing mutations.
- Owner/Admin capability must be resolved through Merchant Access authority, never a client-supplied role/tenant id.
- Concurrency must prevent two accepted overlapping Promotions for the same Tenant + Product under PD-054 interval policy.
- Pricing evaluation failure must fail closed for promotion application; it must never accept a client-computed discount.
- Correlation should allow an accepted Order price to be traced to the authoritative Pricing decision that produced it.
- Runtime remains LocalStack-only under ADR-012.

## Risks and edge cases
- A read-before-write overlap check without protected write semantics is unsafe under concurrency.
- Coupling Sales to mutable Pricing persistence would break historical independence.
- Returning generic discount primitives can accidentally reopen percentage/stacking semantics excluded by PD-054.
- Technical time conversion must not silently pick one side of an ambiguous Tenant-local wall-clock time.
